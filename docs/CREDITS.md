# Credits

DAODream is a personal, non-commercial fan remake. Dragon Age: Origins belongs to BioWare and
Electronic Arts; nothing of the game's own data is in this repository.

Third-party assets used by the project are not in the repository either (they live in the git-ignored
`Content/ThirdParty`, `Content/MetaHumans`, `Content/AnimalVarietyPack` folders and `extracted/`). They
are listed here because their licences ask for credit or because it is fair to name them.
